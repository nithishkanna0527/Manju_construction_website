01
Brand Overview
Chapter One

Brand Personality & Tone

Manju Construction stands for strength, precision, and trust. Every visual and verbal expression must reinforce these core values.

Confident
Speak with authority. Never hedge. Manju Construction has earned its reputation — the brand voice should reflect that certainty.
"We deliver. On time. Every time."
Premium
Every word, every pixel signals quality. Avoid generic corporate language. Choose precise, powerful expressions that feel exclusive.
"Built to outlast generations."
Trustworthy
Transparency builds loyalty. Be direct about processes, timelines, and outcomes. Back statements with proof — numbers, testimonials, years of service.
"20+ years. 100+ projects. 0 compromises."
Clear
No jargon. No filler. Say what needs to be said in the fewest, strongest words. Clients value clarity over complexity.
"One call. Your vision, our expertise."
Aspirational
Speak to what clients want to become — not just what they need today. Elevate expectations and then exceed them.
"Where your dream becomes an address."
Human
Despite the premium positioning, stay approachable. Real people, real homes, real relationships. Warmth beneath the professionalism.
"We build spaces people call home."
✓ Do Say
  • Building Trust. Creating Value.
  • Premium construction, uncompromising quality
  • Your vision, our expertise
  • Built to last generations
  • Precision in every detail
  • Delivered on time, every time
✕ Never Say
  • Cheap rates / affordable options
  • We try our best
  • Quick and easy construction
  • Just another building company
  • Budget-friendly packages
  • We might be able to…

Color Palette
Chapter Two

Color System

Six precision-chosen colors form the entire palette. Never deviate. Never substitute with generic alternatives.

Primary Brand
Brand Red
#E50914
Primary CTAs, key headings, accent highlights, logo "C" element, button backgrounds, section markers. Never use as a body text color.
Luxury Accent
Luxury Gold
#D4AF37
Secondary accents, icon strokes, border treatments, subheadings, secondary buttons, decorative lines. Signals premium and exclusivity.
Foundation
Deep Black
#0D0D0D
Page background, primary surface. The foundation all other elements sit upon. Never use pure #000000 — the 5% lift creates depth.
Elevated Surface
Dark Grey
#1A1A1A
Cards, panels, elevated surfaces. Creates the Z-plane layering system — use to lift elements above the base background.
Supporting Text
Mid Grey
#666666
Body text on dark backgrounds, supporting copy, meta information, placeholder text. Never use for headings or primary content.
Foreground
Off White
#F5F5F5
Primary text on dark backgrounds, headings, UI labels. Use #F5F5F5 — not pure white. The 2% warmth prevents harshness.
Color Usage Ratios
Background · 60%
Surfaces · 25%
Brand Red · 10%
Gold Accent · 5%

Logo Usage
Chapter Four

Logo System

The MC monogram and wordmark are the brand's primary identifiers. Treat them with absolute consistency.

Primary — On Dark
On Light Background
Clear Space
Always maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "M" letterform on all four sides of the logo.
Minimum Size
Digital: minimum 120px wide. Print: minimum 30mm wide. Below these sizes, legibility of the wordmark is compromised.
Approved Backgrounds
Use on Deep Black (#0D0D0D), Dark Grey (#1A1A1A), or white (#F5F5F5) only. No patterned or photographic backgrounds.
✕ Never Do This With The Logo
— Stretch or distort proportions
— Rotate or flip the mark
— Apply drop shadows or glows
— Place on busy backgrounds
— Change the red to another color
— Use partial marks alone
— Add borders or outlines
— Place below minimum size
— Rearrange mark + wordmark

08
Design Principles
Chapter Eight

Core Design Principles

Eight non-negotiable design standards. Every page, every slide, every asset must pass these checks before publication.

01
Minimal But Bold
Restraint is a luxury signal. Use negative space aggressively. Let elements breathe. Then make the elements that DO exist completely unmissable — oversized type, deep contrast, strong shapes.
02
High Contrast Always
Dark backgrounds with gold and red accents. Never place grey on grey. Every text element must meet WCAG AA contrast ratios at minimum. Test in grayscale — if it still works, the contrast is right.
03
Depth & Layering
Three Z-planes: base (#0D0D0D) → surface (#1A1A1A) → floating (#222). Add layered shadows tinted with brand colors. Use radial gradients for light sourcing. Never flat.
04
Trust-First Hierarchy
Lead with credibility signals — years, numbers, certifications — before asking for action. Social proof precedes CTAs. Stats before services. Portfolio before contact form.
05
Purposeful Animation
Animate only transform and opacity. Use spring-style easing (cubic-bezier 0.4,0,0.2,1). Never transition-all. Scroll-triggered reveals create anticipation and premium pacing.
06
Every State Designed
Default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled — all interactive elements have all states. Hover must feel rewarding. Focus rings use gold (#D4AF37). Never skip interactive states.
07
Intentional Typography
Tight tracking (-0.03em) on display headings, generous line-height (1.7) on body. Never use a second font. Establish a clear hierarchy and never break it. Size expresses importance.
08
Gold as Signal
Gold (#D4AF37) is the scarcest color in the palette — use it for the most important secondary information. Section labels, icon strokes, price points. Overuse destroys its premium signal.

✓ Image Criteria
  • High resolution (min 2000px wide)
  • Warm, architectural lighting
  • Clean compositions — rule of thirds
  • Apply gradient overlay (from-black/60)
  • Subtle color grade (warm shadows)
  • Focus on premium materials and finishes
✕ Never Use
  • Stock photos with watermarks
  • Over-saturated or filtered images
  • Low-resolution or blurry photos
  • Images showing construction mess
  • Crowded or chaotic compositions
  • Pure white background product shots
